# Privacy Policy for DateMath

**Last updated:** September 19, 2026

**Developer:** Yusufustically  
**Application:** DateMath  
**Package Name:** com.yusufustically.datemath  

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **DateMath** ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by **Yusufustically**, handles information when you use our mobile application.

Your privacy is extremely important to us. DateMath was designed from the ground up as a **100% offline, privacy-first utility application**. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
**We do not collect any personal data.**  
DateMath does not require an account, registration, login credentials, name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

### B. Dates and Calculation Data
All dates, times, age calculations, day-finder queries, and interval calculations entered into the App are processed **exclusively on your local device in real-time**. This information is never transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

### C. Device and Technical Information
**We do not collect device information.**  
The App does not collect your IP address, IMEI, Android ID, MAC address, advertising identifiers (GAID), or hardware specifications.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & Network Access

* **Zero Internet Permissions:** DateMath does not request or include the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission in its production application manifest.
* **Offline Operation:** The App operates completely without an internet connection and is fully functional in Airplane Mode. It does not communicate with external APIs, servers, or web endpoints.
* **Storage Access:** DateMath does not request access to your device's external storage, photos, media, camera, microphone, or contacts.

---

## 4. Local Data Storage

The App uses local device storage solely to preserve your application preferences:
* **Theme Mode:** Your preference for Light, Dark, or System theme is stored locally on your device via standard Android shared preferences.

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

* **No Analytics:** We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, or any other telemetry tools.
* **No Advertisements:** DateMath does not display ads and does not integrate any third-party ad networks or tracking SDKs.
* **No Social Media Integration:** The App contains no social logins, tracking pixels, or third-party sharing libraries.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

DateMath does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

## 7. Security of Your Data

Because DateMath processes all operations entirely offline and does not transmit data over networks or store personal information on servers, your data is inherently protected from unauthorized remote access or data breaches.
